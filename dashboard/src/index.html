Simulated experiment

Simulates the means of an experiment and decides each pair with a t test, |t| > 1.96. "Methods and sources" below the results gives the model.

Method

CLD-C gives the fewest distinct letters. CLD-sigma gives the fewest assignments of a letter to a group, so the fewest letters in the table. The second stage keeps that minimum and then minimizes the other quantity. It is experimental and exists in this page only.

Methods and sources

Simulated experiments

Treatment means and their covariances are simulated with the model that Section 8 of Ennis, Fayle, and Ennis (2012) uses (Section 3.2 of Gramm et al. 2007; the 2012 paper names it Gramm et al. 2006). A pair differs significantly when |t| > 1.96.

Study

Simulation study

This view follows the design of the experiment of Section 8 of Ennis, Fayle, and Ennis (2012) in this browser. Each instance is one simulated experiment. The page runs CLD-C, CLD-sigma, and both two-stage solves on it, each solve with the time limit of the study settings. The 2012 study ran an exact backtracking search (FIND-AM) on 1,000 instances for each number of treatments, with a limit of 30 seconds.

Set the sizes and the time limit, then press Run the study. The tables appear here as the instances finish.
