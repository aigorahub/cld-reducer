Input

This page reads significance decisions. It does not run significance tests. A group is one item or treatment.

Examples

Simulated experiment

Treatment means and their covariances are simulated with the model that Section 8 of Ennis, Fayle, and Ennis (2012) uses (Gramm et al. 2007, Section 3.2). A pair differs significantly when |t| > 1.96.

One row for each pair of groups, with the columns group1, group2, and significant. The decision is true when the two groups differ significantly. Every pair needs a row.

or .

Columns group and mean. The means set the order of the groups and of the letters. They do not change a decision.

Method

CLD-C gives the fewest distinct letters. CLD-sigma gives the fewest assignments of a letter to a group. The second stage keeps that minimum and then minimizes the other quantity. It is experimental and exists in this page only.

Simulation study

This view repeats the experiment of Section 8 of Ennis, Fayle, and Ennis (2012) in this browser. Each instance is one simulated experiment. The page runs CLD-C, CLD-sigma, and both two-stage solves on it, each solve with the time limit below. The 2012 study ran an exact backtracking search (FIND-AM) on 1,000 instances for each number of treatments, with a limit of 30 seconds.
